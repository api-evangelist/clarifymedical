Clarifymedical is a healthcare data analytics company that provides APIs for patient activation, growth intelligence, and health system orchestration. It offers services such as Meridian and Arrival to help health systems understand and improve care delivery, leveraging comprehensive health economics data. The company focuses on integrating patient data, provider contracts, and network analytics to drive better health outcomes and operational efficiency.

**Areas:** Company · Healthcare · Data Analytics · Health Tech
